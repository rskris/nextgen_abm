## Requirements

### Requirement: US-101 Peak-Spreading Scenario Evaluation
The system SHALL evaluate regional traffic flow, peak flattening, and schedule adjustments under employer flexible work hours and telework policy scenarios on the US-101 corridor.

#### Scenario: Simulating 2-hour flexible arrival window
- **WHEN** South Coast major employers adopt flexible start times between 07:30 and 09:30 AM
- **THEN** the model reports the simulated shift in departure times, the flattening of US-101 morning peak vehicle volumes, and the ripple effects on secondary afternoon activities.

### Requirement: Clock-Face Regional Rail Transformation Evaluation
The system SHALL evaluate mode shifting, vehicle shedding, and off-peak travel patterns under clock-face 30/60-minute service frequency scenarios for Amtrak Pacific Surfliner and local transit feeder pulses.

#### Scenario: Hourly Pacific Surfliner rail scenario
- **WHEN** Pacific Surfliner service is simulated with 60-minute clock-face headways between Santa Maria, Goleta, Santa Barbara, and Ventura
- **THEN** the model quantifies the reduction in single-occupancy vehicle trips on US-101 and the percentage of multi-worker households able to transition from 2 vehicles to 1 vehicle.

### Requirement: Higher-Education Staggered Class Timetable Evaluation
The system SHALL simulate the impact of staggered university lecture start times on campus bicycle roundabout congestion, pedestrian pathway volumes, and transit boarding queues.

#### Scenario: Staggering UCSB lecture hours
- **WHEN** UCSB class schedules are split between top-of-the-hour and half-hour start blocks
- **THEN** the model calculates the reduction in peak 10-minute bicycle flow rates entering campus from Isla Vista and boarding queues on MTD lines 24x and 11.
